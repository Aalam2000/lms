# How to Work as a Curator

The curator brings students to the academy and hands them over to the teachers.

## Main

- At the top, select the **sector** and **course** using the buttons (pressing again deselects) and **teacher** from the list — the list includes teachers who have groups with that sector and course.
- Below is the **lesson calendar** for this teacher. The teacher's days off are highlighted in red.
- Click on the lesson you created — it can be **rescheduled** to another date and time and **opened or closed** for students. The teacher works inside the lesson (journal, materials, Homework). After midnight on the lesson day and on the teacher's day off, rescheduling is not allowed.
- **+ Add Student** — new student:
  1. **First and last name** (first name first) and **student's phone number** — required. Parent's information — if possible.
  2. **Course** — required. **Teacher** and **group** — optional. If a teacher is specified, the student will immediately appear in their "Students," even without a group.
  3. Checkbox **Immediately additional lesson** — personal lesson in the selected group: for this, the group is required. A lesson cannot be scheduled on the teacher's day off.

After saving, the student's card will open: **Student Access** (address, login, and password with **Copy** and **Send via WhatsApp** buttons) and **Link for Parent**.

## Students

All students of the academy — just like in the administrator's view: filters by teacher, course, and group, grades, absences, contacts. A student who has not yet been enrolled in a group is marked as "without group."

- **Pencil** — student and parent data.
- **Key** — change password.
- **Additional lesson** — personal lesson for the student; if they do not have a group, select one — the student will enroll in it.

You add new students and enrol them in a group. A student who already studies in another group is enrolled only by the administrator.
- Student's name — report on them.

## Rooms

In the [Rooms](/dashboard/rooms) section — a directory of the academy's rooms: **number** (only a number, not repeated) and **number of seats** for students. You can add, edit (pencil), and delete a room. The directory is maintained by you and the administrator.

## Logins and Passwords

- A new student's login is created automatically: first name, dot, first three letters of the last name, and numbers — for example, `murad.ali47`. The password is initially the same as the login. If the student changes the password and forgets it — **Reset to login** in the card.
- A parent can log in via a link or by their own login: `p.` and the child's login, the password is also the same as the login.

## Profile

In the [Profile](/dashboard/profile) you can change your password.
