# How to view your child's performance

The Teacher has sent you a personal link. Open it — no password is needed. It is convenient to open the link from your phone.

## What is on the page

- If you have several children in the academy, there are tabs at the top with their names. Click on the name to view the performance of that child.
- **Average grades** — for lessons, for homework, and for exams. Click on the tile to open a list of all grades.
- **Lessons** — each lesson with the date: whether the child was present, was late, grades for the lesson, Homework, and Exam.
- **Absences** — missed lessons. "Absence closed with an additional lesson" means that the child attended an additional lesson and the absence does not count.

## Lesson details

Click on the lesson — a window will open:

- **Homework** — assignment file and deadline. Click on the file to open it.
- **Student's response** — whether the child submitted the Homework, their files, and the Teacher's grade.
- **Dialogue** — correspondence between the Teacher and the student regarding this lesson.

## Important

- You can only view the page: you cannot change grades or send messages. Questions — to the Teacher.
- Do not forward the link to outsiders: it shows the child's performance. If the link has reached the wrong person, ask the Teacher to create a new one — the old one will stop working.
