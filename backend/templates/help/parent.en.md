# How to view your child's academic performance

The teacher has sent you a personal link. Open it — no password is needed. It is convenient to open the link from your phone.

Along with the link, the teacher may have sent the platform address, username, and password. By logging in with the username, you will reach the same page. The password can be changed; if forgotten, ask the teacher to reset it.

## What is on the page

- If you have several children in the academy, the tabs at the top show their names. Click on the name to view the academic performance of that child.
- **Average grades** — for lessons, for homework, and for exams. Click on the tile to open the list of all grades.
- **Lessons** — each lesson with the date: whether the child attended the lesson, was late, grades for the lesson, Homework, and Exam.
- **Absences** — missed lessons. "Absence covered by an additional lesson" means that the child attended an additional lesson, and the absence does not count.

## Lesson details

Click on the lesson — a window will open:

- **Homework** — the assignment file and the deadline. Click on the file to open it.
- **Student's response** — whether the child submitted the Homework, their files, and the teacher's grade.
- **Dialogue** — correspondence between the teacher and the student regarding this lesson.

## Important

- You can only view the page: you cannot change grades or send messages. Questions should be directed to the teacher.
- Do not forward the link to outsiders: it shows the child's academic performance. If the link has reached the wrong person, ask the teacher to create a new one — the old one will stop working.
