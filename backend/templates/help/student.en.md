# How to work on the platform

## Home — my lessons

If you just registered via QR, it says "Waiting for the teacher to confirm" on the Home page. As soon as the teacher accepts you into the group, your lessons will appear here.

On the [Home](/dashboard) — all your lessons. At the top — the name of your group and the button to enter the video conference.

Lessons can be viewed in two ways:

- **Table** — a list of lessons opened by the teacher. Today's lesson is marked "Today."
- **Calendar** — all group lessons by dates. Gray lessons have not yet been opened by the teacher. Red days are the teacher's days off.

A lesson where you need to do something is highlighted:

- **Homework: submit** (📝) — the homework has not been submitted yet;
- **Homework: revise** (↺) — the teacher returned the answer for revision;
- **Messages** (💬) — new messages from the teacher.

Click on the lesson to open it.

If you have unsubmitted homework, a colored bar with the lesson name and deadline is visible under the header on any page: orange — homework is waiting for a response or returned for revision, red — the deadline has passed. The link **Open lesson** leads directly to the assignment. The bar disappears as soon as you attach your response. Assignments that the group received before your arrival are not considered overdue — but if you complete them, the grade will count.

## Lesson

When the lesson starts, a question **"How do you like the lesson?"** appears at the top right along with three smileys: green — liked it, yellow — okay, red — didn't like it. Click on one of them. The rating can be changed. The teacher does not see who selected which smiley.

A lesson marked 👤 — is personal: only you and those invited by the teacher can see it.

Below are four tabs: **Materials**, **Work during the lesson**, **Homework** and **Dialogue**. The number on the tab indicates that something is waiting for you there; on the "Work during the lesson" tab — how many files you have submitted.

### Materials

Files and links for the lesson. Click on the title — the material will open in a new tab. Below the list — teacher's notes for the lesson.

### Work in Class

This is where you submit what the teacher asked you to do in class.

1. Click **Upload File** or drag files into the box. You can upload several at once.
2. While the teacher hasn't checked the work, you can delete an extra file (✕).
3. When the teacher checks it, **Accepted** or **Grade** will appear — this is the grade for the lesson. You cannot change the work after it has been checked.

You can submit the work while the lesson is open and until the end of the lesson day.

### Homework

1. Click on the assignment title — the file will download. The deadline is indicated next to it.
2. Complete the assignment and click **+ File** to upload your answer. You can upload several files.
3. While the teacher hasn't checked the answer and the deadline hasn't passed, you can delete the file (✕) and upload a new one.

Answer status:

- **Not submitted** — you haven't uploaded an answer yet;
- **Waiting for review** — the answer is with the teacher;
- **Returned for revision** — correct it and upload a new file, details in the "Dialogue";
- **Accepted** or **Grade** — the answer has been checked, you cannot change it anymore;
- **Not submitted, deadline expired** — the deadline has passed.

### Dialogue

Correspondence with the teacher regarding this lesson. Write a message and send it. You can edit your message — the **Edit** button.

## Performance

In the [Performance](/dashboard/performance) section — all your grades for lessons, homework, and exams, as well as absences. You can see the grades for each lesson here — they are not on the lesson page. An absence can be closed: if you attended an additional (personal) lesson after it, it is marked as "Absence closed" and does not count against you.

## Profile

In the [Profile](/dashboard/profile) you can change the password.
